What this round tests: how much design should leak into content editing

Three new client-editor prototypes sit on one deliberate axis. Same example site, same words — only the amount of your design that reaches the writing surface changes. The goal is to find the sweet spot where clients feel safe and capable, without mistaking the editor for the finished page.

  • NoneClean Slate exposes no design at all. The agency owns every visual decision.
  • Purpose-levelPurpose Blocks offers design as intent — “this section’s job” — with optional peeks, never CSS.
  • GatedLayered Canvas keeps content clean, then divides design into its own explicit, locked mode.
